# Synthetic Company Onboarding Policy
*Notice: This is synthetic challenge data for OnboardFlow AI demonstration.*

## General Welcome and Compliance
All incoming employees regardless of location or employment type must complete standard Day 1 company compliance:
- Sign Employee Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) and Intellectual Property Agreement.
- Submit statutory identity and tax documents (I-9 / PAN / Aadhaar).
- Complete mandatory Code of Business Conduct acknowledgement within 48 hours of joining.

## HR Orientation Session
- Human Resources (HR) conducts a mandatory 60-minute company orientation session on the employee's first morning at 10:00 AM local time.
- HR introduces company culture, organizational structure, benefit enrolment windows, and primary point of contacts.
