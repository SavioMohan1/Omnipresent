# Synthetic Security and Physical Access Policy
*Notice: This is synthetic challenge data for OnboardFlow AI demonstration.*

## Physical Facility Access & RFID Smart Badges
- The Security team is responsible for verifying identity documents and issuing an RFID Smart Access Badge on Day 1.
- Physical badges are required for all onsite and hybrid employees to enter turnstiles, elevators, and general office floors.
- Temporary visitor passes expire within 24 hours; permanent badges must be programmed within 4 hours of arrival.

## High-Security R&D Lab Access
- Access to the Hardware and Systems Engineering Lab requires explicit Security clearance and manager sponsorship.
- Prerequisites: General physical badge must already be active before engineering lab access can be granted.
- Sales, marketing, and non-technical personnel are strictly prohibited from entering secure lab zones without escort.

## Information Security & Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)
- Every employee must register hardware security keys (FIDO2/WebAuthn) or Microsoft Authenticator before accessing internal repositories.
- Security conducts a mandatory Phishing and Cyber Hygiene training module due within 5 calendar days.
