# Synthetic IT Provisioning Policy
*Notice: This is synthetic challenge data for OnboardFlow AI demonstration.*

## Hardware Equipment Allocation
- Software Engineering & Data roles receive high-performance workstations (Apple MacBook Pro M3 Max 36GB or Lenovo ThinkPad P1 64GB Linux/Windows).
- Sales, HR, and Operations roles receive lightweight corporate laptops (Apple MacBook Air M3 16GB or Dell XPS 13 16GB).
- IT Operations provisions and stages the machine 48 hours prior to start date, encrypting disks with BitLocker/FileVault.

## Corporate Identity, Email, and Single Sign-On (SSO)
- IT creates the corporate Microsoft 365 identity, mailbox, and Entra ID Single Sign-On account.
- Identity and corporate email must be activated before department-specific software or cloud console access can be provisioned.

## VPN and Remote Network Access
- IT installs GlobalProtect Enterprise VPN client and zero-trust device posture agents.
- Hybrid and remote employees require corporate VPN certificates to reach staging clusters and internal wikis.
